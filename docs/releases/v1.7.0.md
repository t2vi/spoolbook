# spoolbook v1.7.0 — a new look, and dark mode

**Released**: 2026-10-16

MINOR: a light/dark switch and a breadcrumb top bar are new capabilities; the rest is a
restyle. Nothing breaking. Shipped as a hotfix release straight to `main`.

Spec coverage: 100% of surfaces, 91% of FRs tested (70% at planned layer).

## Added

- **Dark mode.** The moon/sun button at the top right switches between light and dark. Until
  you choose, it follows your system setting; your choice is remembered in that browser. (#245)
- **Breadcrumb.** A top bar shows where you are — home, the sidebar group and the page, then
  *New*, *Edit* or *Details* on a sub-page (e.g. 🏠 › Filaments › List › Edit). (#245)

## Changed

A new look inspired by the Pinx admin template (#245):

- Light gray page with white cards, soft borders, rounder corners and an emerald accent.
- The sidebar highlights the page you're on (it didn't before); Settings and your account sit
  in a panel at the bottom.
- Dashboard: count cards with an icon each and large numbers; spools by status, prints by
  outcome, and top brands and materials as cards with colored share bars.
- Tables have a tinted header row and roomier rows.
- Status badges — print outcome, spool status, printer connection, camera — are tinted pills
  instead of solid colors.

## Fixed

- Pages rendered narrower than intended (each shrank to its content), most visibly the Printers
  header, where **+ Add printer** sat against the title.

## For developers

- New e2e test `e2e/topbar.e2e.ts` (002/FR-009). Shared badge variants `success`, `warning`,
  `danger`, `info`, `neutral` and `printStatusVariant` replace four copies of a status-color
  helper.

## Upgrading

No manual step. Pull the new image and restart:

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```

Closes #245.
